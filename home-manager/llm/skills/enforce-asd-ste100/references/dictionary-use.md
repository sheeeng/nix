# Dictionary Use

## Purpose

Part 2 of Issue 9 is a controlled dictionary. An entry can approve a word only for a specified part of speech, meaning, and set of forms. Lowercase entries identify words that are not approved and give approved alternatives. Uppercase entries identify approved words.

## Validate a Word

1. Identify the intended meaning and part of speech in the sentence.
2. Find the word in an authorized Issue 9 dictionary.
3. If the entry is uppercase, confirm that the approved meaning and part of speech match the sentence.
4. For a verb or adjective, confirm that the form is approved.
5. If the entry is lowercase, use an approved alternative or rewrite the sentence.
6. Do not replace a word mechanically. Rewrite the complete sentence when the alternative has different grammar.
7. If the word is absent, determine whether it is an approved technical noun or technical verb. Otherwise, do not use it.

## Technical Terms

Use the project glossary or terminology database as the authority for technical nouns and technical verbs. Apply these controls:

1. Assign one term to one concept.
2. Use the same term for every occurrence of that concept.
3. Do not use a technical noun as a verb.
4. Do not use a technical verb as a noun.
5. Prefer a short, clear term when the subject field permits a choice.
6. Do not introduce regional, slang, or jargon variants.
7. Record an approved long term, its short form, and its abbreviation before use.

## Report the Review

Use full compliance language only when you checked the text with the complete Issue 9 dictionary and the applicable project terminology source.

When either source is unavailable, report one of these limitations:

1. Dictionary validation not complete.
2. Project terminology validation not complete.
3. Writing rules applied; lexical compliance not verified.

Do not present a general English spell checker, style checker, or language model judgment as dictionary validation.
